# HelpBnk Step 3 - Smoke-Test Landing Page (Free, Pro GBP 7, Pro+ GBP 12)
**Date:** February 20, 2026

## Objective
Validate demand and price sensitivity before scaling product build.

## Hypothesis
1. High-frequency sellers will engage with a clear value proposition around faster comps and profit clarity.
2. At least 8-12% of active users will show paid intent at GBP 7 or GBP 12 plans.

## Landing Page Structure
1. Hero: "Scan anything. Price faster. Buy smarter."
2. Core outcomes: instant valuation, confidence score, risk indicators, profit view
3. Plan cards: Free, Pro GBP 7, Pro+ GBP 12
4. Email capture + CTA buttons for each plan
5. FAQ + trust section

## Tracking Setup
Track:
1. Page visits
2. Email signups
3. Clicks by plan (Free / GBP 7 / GBP 12)
4. Conversion rate from visitor to signup
5. Objections from replies/messages

## Distribution Plan
1. Share to high-frequency seller communities
2. Direct outreach to target users (DM/email)
3. Post short video/demo snippet linked to page

## Success Criteria (14 days)
1. 100+ targeted visits
2. 20+ email signups
3. Clear signal of plan preference (GBP 7 vs GBP 12)
4. Top 3 objections documented with exact user wording

## Decision Rule
1. Strong GBP 7 pull + acceptable intent at GBP 12 -> continue pricing test and onboarding
2. Weak paid pull -> revise positioning/paywall and rerun test

## Output / Completion Criteria
1. One-page summary: traffic, signup %, plan click split
2. Top objections and pricing feedback
3. Recommendation: keep, adjust, or pivot pricing

# HelpBnk Step 25 - Public Launch with Focused Channels and Proofs
**Date:** February 21, 2026

## Objective
Launch publicly with focused channels, clear message, and credible proof.

## Channel Plan
1. eBay seller communities
2. Relevant Discord/Facebook groups
3. Founder social posts with demo clips
4. Direct outreach to warm leads

## Launch Content
1. Crisp value proposition
2. 30-60 second demo
3. Clear CTA (join, scan, or trial)
4. Early case study/profit proof

## What To Collect
1. Testimonials and user quotes
2. First public conversion metrics
3. Common objections from new users

## Output
1. Launch summary with channel performance
2. Updated channel strategy based on ROI
3. Public proof assets for investor/sales use

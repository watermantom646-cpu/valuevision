## What ValueVision Does
ValueVision is an all-in-one mobile app that helps users scan items and make faster, safer decisions.  
The app returns:
1. Estimated value range
2. Risk/authenticity indicators
3. Confidence score
4. Clear next-step guidance

It is built for multi-category use, including vehicles, electronics, fashion, collectibles, tools, and general resale items.

## Problem
People currently rely on fragmented tools and unclear data when buying or selling second-hand items.  
This causes:
1. Pricing mistakes
2. Hidden risk
3. Slow, low-confidence decisions

## Solution
One workflow: **Scan -> Understand -> Decide**

ValueVision combines scan detection, market signals, and risk checks into one mobile-first experience.

# HelpBnk Step 21 - Lock the Soft-Launch Offer for High-Frequency eBay Sellers
**Date:** February 21, 2026

## Objective
Finalize a clear, compelling soft-launch offer with concrete limits and upgrade rules.

## What To Define
1. Offer promise in one sentence
2. Feature limits for Free / Pro / Pro+
3. Trial rules and upgrade moments
4. Incentives for early adopters

## What To Do
1. Draft one-page offer copy.
2. Finalize tier limits and prompt timing.
3. Align channels (communities, DMs, email list).
4. Approve incentives (discount, bonus scans, founder access).

## Output
1. Final soft-launch one-pager
2. Offer copy ready for landing/app/social
3. Internal checklist for consistent messaging

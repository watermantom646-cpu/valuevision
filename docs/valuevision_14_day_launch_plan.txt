1) Revenue Model (Simple)
------------------------------
- Price: GBP 7/month (Pro)
- Free tier: 5 scans/day
- Trial: 7-day free trial
- Paywall trigger: after free scan limit, or after first 3 profitable scans shown
- Core paid promise:
  1. Faster live scan
  2. Profit calculator
  3. Sell-time estimate
  4. Where-to-sell recommendations
  5. Listing assistant

Target by Day 14:

2) KPI Dashboard (Track Daily)
------------------------------
- Activation: % users who complete first scan
- Accuracy trust: % scans with confidence medium/high
- Conversion: free -> trial, trial -> paid
- D7 retention
- Avg scans/user/day
- Timeout rate (live mode)
- "No pricing" rate

Target thresholds:
- Activation >= 65%
- Timeout rate <= 10%
- "No pricing" <= 15%
- Trial -> paid >= 25%

# ValueVision - Pitch Deck Script (10 Slides)

## Slide 1 - Cover
**Title:** ValueVision  
**Subtitle:** Scan Anything. Verify Faster. Decide Smarter.  
**Say:** "We’re building an all-in-one mobile decision tool for second-hand buying and selling."

## Slide 2 - Problem
**Title:** The Decision Problem  
**Bullets:**
1. Data is fragmented across tools
2. Users miss risk signals
3. Pricing confidence is low
4. Decisions are slow and stressful
**Say:** "People are forced to piece together trust, value, and risk from multiple places."

## Slide 3 - Solution
**Title:** One Scan, One Clear Report  
**Bullets:**
1. Scan or upload item photo
2. AI/data checks run in seconds
3. Value + risk + confidence displayed clearly
**Say:** "We turn complexity into one simple decision flow."

## Slide 4 - Product
**Title:** What ValueVision Delivers  
**Bullets:**
1. Estimated value range
2. Risk/authenticity indicators
3. Confidence scoring
4. Action guidance (buy/sell/pass)
**Say:** "It’s decision support, not raw data overload."

## Slide 5 - Multi-Category Advantage
**Title:** Not Just Cars  
**Bullets:**
1. Vehicles
2. Electronics
3. Fashion/luxury
4. Collectibles
5. Tools/home/general resale
**Say:** "The same workflow scales across categories, making this a broad utility product."

## Slide 6 - Market + Users
**Title:** Who Uses It  
**Bullets:**
1. Private buyers
2. Resellers/flippers
3. Small dealers
4. Small business operators
**Say:** "We start with high-frequency decision users and expand from there."

## Slide 7 - Business Model
**Title:** Monetisation  
**Bullets:**
1. Freemium access
2. Monthly subscription for advanced reports
3. B2B tooling later
**Say:** "Revenue starts with subscriptions, then expands into B2B."

## Slide 8 - Traction / Current State
**Title:** Execution So Far  
**Bullets:**
1. MVP and proof of concept built
2. Core backend/app running
3. Early access funnel being launched
4. Beta onboarding in progress
**Say:** "We’re now in milestone-driven execution mode."

## Slide 9 - The Raise
**Title:** Raising GBP 20k - GBP 30k  
**Use of Funds:**
1. Data/API costs
2. Product reliability and UX hardening
3. Launch + early growth testing
4. Ops/legal/compliance
**Say:** "This is validation capital to de-risk and prove repeatable traction."

## Slide 10 - 90-Day Milestones + Ask
**Title:** What This Capital Unlocks  
**Milestones:**
1. Private beta launch and active cohort
2. Reliability KPI targets reached
3. First paying users
4. Investor update with usage/retention/conversion metrics
**Ask:** "Looking for aligned angel/advisor support and strategic introductions."

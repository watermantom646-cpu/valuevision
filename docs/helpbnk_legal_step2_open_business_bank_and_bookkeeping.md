# HelpBnk Legal Step 2 - Open Business Bank Account and Simple Bookkeeping
**Date:** February 21, 2026

## Objective
Set clean business financial operations from day one.

## Bank Setup
1. Open business bank account in company name
2. Keep all business transactions separate from personal spending
3. Enable cards/expense controls for business-only use

## Bookkeeping Setup
1. Connect bank feed to Xero
2. Create simple chart of accounts
3. Tag spending categories (API costs, infra, software, marketing, legal)
4. Save receipts/invoices weekly

## Financial Controls
1. Monthly profit/loss snapshot
2. Cash runway tracker
3. Weekly spend review against budget

## Completion Criteria
1. Business account active
2. Xero connected and categorization live
3. Weekly bookkeeping routine established

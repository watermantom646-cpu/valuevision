## What We Do
ValueVision is an all-in-one mobile app that lets users scan items and instantly get clear decision insights:
1. Estimated value range
2. Risk/authenticity signals
3. Confidence score
4. Simple next-step recommendation

It supports multiple categories including vehicles, electronics, fashion, collectibles, tools, and general resale items.

## Problem
Buyers and sellers currently use fragmented tools and unclear data, causing:
1. Slow decision-making
2. Pricing mistakes
3. Hidden risk
4. Low trust in second-hand transactions

## Solution
One workflow: **Scan -> Understand -> Decide**

ValueVision combines detection + market context + risk indicators into one mobile-first report.

## Why Now
1. Growth in second-hand commerce
2. Increased buyer caution and fraud concern
3. Mobile-first behavior for purchase decisions

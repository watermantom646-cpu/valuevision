# HelpBnk Legal Step 5 - Protect Brand and IP; Set Contractor and NDA Templates
**Date:** February 21, 2026

## Objective
Protect the business brand/IP and standardize legal paperwork for collaboration.

## Brand Protection
1. Secure core domain and handles
2. Start trademark review for chosen brand name/logo
3. Keep brand usage guidelines simple and documented

## IP Protection
1. Confirm company ownership of core code/assets
2. Use IP assignment clauses in all contractor agreements
3. Keep sensitive logic and credentials controlled

## Template Pack
Prepare standard templates for:
1. Mutual NDA
2. Contractor agreement with IP assignment
3. Advisor agreement (if applicable)
4. Data-sharing/confidentiality clause for external reviews

## Completion Criteria
1. Template pack ready for use
2. IP ownership position documented
3. Brand protection steps initiated and tracked

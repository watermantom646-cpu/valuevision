# HelpBnk Legal Step 1 - Choose UK Structure, Name, and Register Company
**Date:** February 21, 2026

## Objective
Set the correct legal structure and complete UK company registration properly.

## Structure Decision
Recommended starting structure:
1. Private Limited Company (Ltd)
2. Founder as director and shareholder
3. Separate personal and business liabilities

## Name and Availability Checks
1. Check Companies House name availability
2. Check domain and social handle availability
3. Reserve consistent brand naming across key channels

## Registration Actions
1. Register company with Companies House
2. Register for HMRC corporation tax
3. Set registered office and director details correctly
4. Store certificate of incorporation and filings in one folder

## SIC Code Direction (initial)
Use software/app-related SIC code(s) appropriate to product activity.

## Completion Criteria
1. Company legally registered
2. Name secured across Companies House + domain
3. Core registration documents stored and accessible

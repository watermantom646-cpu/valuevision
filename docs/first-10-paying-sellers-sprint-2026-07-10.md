# ValueVision First 10 Paying Sellers Sprint

Date: 10 July 2026
Duration: 14 days from checkout activation
Primary outcome: 10 completed GBP 9.99 payments and 10 activated founding access codes

## The Offer

ValueVision Founding Seller Beta gives a reseller 25 item scans for 30 days. Each scan aims to provide item identity, relevant market evidence, a realistic resale range, confidence, profit guidance, and a listing draft. Car checks are paused and are not included.

## Ideal First Customer

Prioritize a seller who:

- Lists or sells at least 10 second-hand items per month.
- Sources mixed stock from car boot sales, charity shops, auctions, house clearances, or marketplace bundles.
- Currently searches sold listings manually before buying or pricing.
- Uses eBay as a primary or secondary marketplace.
- Is willing to share examples where a result helps or fails.
- Is based in the UK during the founding beta.

Avoid spending early outreach time on people who rarely sell, want formal appraisals, only value cars, or expect every specialist collectible to be priced from one unclear photograph.

## Daily Schedule

### Day 1: Prepare

1. Enable the hosted checkout and access-code backend secrets.
2. Confirm the public demo opens directly into Anything Mode.
3. Record one clear 30-second demo using a normal resale item.
4. Build a private list of 50 qualified UK sellers.
5. Prepare ten unassigned access codes in the private customer tracker.

### Days 2 to 4: Start Conversations

1. Send 20 personal messages per day.
2. Comment helpfully on five relevant seller posts before messaging.
3. Offer a short live demonstration using one of the seller's own items.
4. Record every reply, objection, demo, and payment.
5. Update the offer wording only when the same confusion appears repeatedly.

### Days 5 to 7: Demonstrate Value

1. Run at least two demonstrations per day.
2. Show the complete decision, not only the price estimate.
3. Ask what the seller would have done without ValueVision.
4. Ask whether the result saved time, prevented a bad buy, or improved a listing.
5. Request payment only after the seller has seen a relevant result.

### Day 8: Review the Funnel

1. Count messages, replies, demos, checkouts, payments, activations, and scans.
2. Identify the channel producing the most demos.
3. Identify the most common reason people do not pay.
4. Stop using any message that produces replies but no demos.
5. Keep the price stable unless the offer itself is clearly misunderstood.

### Days 9 to 12: Focus the Winning Channel

1. Send another 20 targeted messages per day using the strongest proof from week one.
2. Publish one useful scan demonstration per day.
3. Ask active founding users for one introduction to another seller.
4. Personally help each new customer complete a first scan.
5. Resolve onboarding problems before adding product features.

Activated founding sellers receive a public `VVREF-...` referral link in the app. Ask them to share that link rather than their private access code so introductions are attributed safely.

### Days 13 and 14: Close and Learn

1. Follow up once with every seller who watched a demo but did not decide.
2. Interview every paying user for ten minutes.
3. Ask every paying user whether they would renew at GBP 9.99.
4. Summarize the three strongest outcomes and three largest product failures.
5. Decide whether the next milestone should be 25 customers or a product correction.

## Thirty-Second Video Script

1. Show the item and say what it cost or what the seller is considering paying.
2. Take or upload the photograph.
3. Show the identification and market evidence.
4. Show the resale range and expected profit.
5. Show the listing draft and saved result.
6. Close with: `ValueVision helps resellers decide what to buy, what to charge, and how to list it.`

## Five-Minute Demo

1. Ask where the seller normally sources stock.
2. Ask how they currently research an unfamiliar item.
3. Use one real item from that seller.
4. Explain confidence and why ValueVision may request more details.
5. Compare the result with the seller's existing knowledge.
6. Save the item and show the listing draft.
7. Ask whether this would change a real buying or pricing decision.

## Objection Responses

### I can use eBay sold listings for free

> You should keep using sold listings. ValueVision is meant to reduce the manual work by identifying the item from a photo, narrowing the evidence, showing confidence, estimating potential profit, and preparing the listing in one flow.

### What if the price is wrong?

> It is resale guidance rather than a formal appraisal. The app shows confidence and evidence, and it asks for more details when an exact result would be unsafe. Founding users also have a direct way to report uncertain results.

### Why is it limited to 25 scans?

> The beta has a clear allowance so we can keep API spending controlled while measuring the true cost and value of each completed scan. We will use that evidence to design the long-term plans.

### Can it check cars?

> Car checks are paused while provider costs are being reset. The founding beta is focused on normal resale inventory because that is the strongest and most affordable launch product.

## Go or Correct Decision

Proceed toward 25 paying sellers when all of the following are true:

- At least 10 strangers have paid.
- At least 8 have activated access and completed a scan.
- At least 5 returned within seven days.
- At least 5 say they are likely to renew.
- API costs remain within the planned margin.
- No repeated result-quality issue creates an unacceptable trust risk.
- Helpful feedback materially exceeds needs-review feedback.

If payments happen but usage does not, fix onboarding and product value before increasing outreach. If demos happen but payments do not, improve the offer or target customer before adding features. If messages do not produce demos, change the channel and proof rather than lowering the price immediately.

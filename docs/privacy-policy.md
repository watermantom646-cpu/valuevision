# ValueVision Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ValueVision ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the ValueVision mobile application and related services (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Account/contact information you provide (for example, email).
- Item scan content you submit (for example, photos and text descriptions).
- Valuation request metadata (for example, item category, condition, region, timestamps).
- Device/app usage information (for example, app version, basic diagnostics, performance logs).
- Transaction and subscription records (if paid plans are enabled).

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data beyond what is required to provide the Service.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide valuations and confidence ranges.
- Improve pricing accuracy and model quality.
- Maintain service reliability, security, and fraud prevention.
- Provide customer support.
- Communicate product and account updates.
- Meet legal obligations.

## 3. Data Sources and Third Parties

To provide valuation results, we may process data using third-party services (for example, marketplace and image-analysis providers).

These providers may process limited data strictly for service delivery. We do not sell user personal data.

## 4. Legal Basis (Where Applicable)

Where required by law, we process data under one or more of:

- Performance of a contract (to provide the Service).
- Legitimate interests (product improvement, fraud prevention, security).
- Consent (where required).
- Legal obligations.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary to:

- Provide the Service.
- Support pricing quality and auditability.
- Comply with law.

Typical retention periods:

- Scan records and valuation history: up to 24 months unless deleted earlier.
- Diagnostic logs: up to 12 months.
- Billing records: as legally required.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access your data.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your data.
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Data portability.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact: **support@valuevisionapp.com**

## 7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or higher age where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and secure secret handling. No system is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date and, where required, provide notice.
